What We Do

Our Professional Services

We deliver exceptional contracting solutions across five core residential services. Every project is executed with premium materials and skilled craftsmanship.

Renovations

Complete home, kitchen, and bathroom transformations. We manage layout design, structural adjustments, and high-end finishes to modernize your living space.

  • Kitchen remodeling
  • Bathroom upgrades
  • Basement finishing

Fencing

Custom boundary solutions that blend security, privacy, and curb appeal. Built to last against local weather using select premium materials.

  • Cedar privacy fences
  • Modern horizontal slatted fences
  • Custom gates & hardware

Building

Structural carpentry, additions, framing, and custom outdoor decks. We construct solid, code-compliant structures that extend your home's functionality.

  • Timber deck construction
  • Structural house framing
  • Home additions & pergolas

Flooring

Premium floor installations and restorations. From rich hardwoods to clean tiling, we ensure flat, durable, and beautiful underfoot surfaces.

  • Hardwood & engineered flooring
  • Luxury vinyl plank (LVP)
  • Custom tile & stone work

Installer Services

Precision installation of high-value residential fixtures, custom cabinetry, windows, doors, and hardware to complete your home interior.

  • Custom cabinet installation
  • Window & door trim-out
  • Finish carpentry & fixtures
